CHRONICLES OF THE REALM

Field Journals & Archival Excerpts from the Expeditionary Guild

The Lost Armory of Karak-Drak

"On the ninth day of the deep winter excavation, our lantern light caught the glint of rune-chiseled steel. Embedded in the petrified basalt of the lower vault sat the Ironbeard War Hammer, unmarred by the collapses that brought low the dwarf kings a millennium ago. We wrapped it in oiled hides, for the runes still pulsed with heat whenever thunder rolled overhead."

— Master Excavator Brom, 3rd Guild Expedition

The Sea-King's Sunken Longship

"Three leagues off the jagged reefs of the northern fjords, the diving bells brought up the round oak shield of Jarl Haakon. Though two hundred winters have passed under saltwater, the raw cowhide and forged iron boss remain steadfast. The bind-runes carved into the wood seem to turn away even sea brine and decay."

— Captain Laurence, Corsair of the White Gull

The Celestial Hermit's Elixirs

"Deep in the moss-draped canopy of Briarwood, we discovered the alchemist's hand-blown phials. Within the glass swirled a midnight-blue fluid containing what could only be described as condensed starlight. When the cork was loosened, a fragrance of alpine frost and dried honeysuckle filled the cavern."

— Sister Martha, Herbalist of the Order
